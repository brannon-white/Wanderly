# Privacy Policy for Wanderly

**Effective date: June 5, 2026**

Wanderly ("Wanderly", "we", "us", or "our"), provided by **Brannon White**, operates the Wanderly mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you create an account using email/password or Google Sign-In, we collect your name and email address. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

**Profile and preferences.** To personalize your itineraries, we collect the travel and food preferences you provide, the taste profile we infer from your in-app swipes and selections, and the trip details and prompts you enter (such as destination, dates, party size, budget, and free-text descriptions of what you want).

**Generated content.** We store the itineraries generated for you and your saved and committed trips.

**Purchase information.** When you buy a subscription or trip credits, the transaction is processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your purchase and subscription status (for example, whether you have an active subscription and how many credits you hold). We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

**Usage and analytics.** We use Firebase Analytics to collect app-usage events — such as when an itinerary is generated, when the paywall is shown, and when a purchase is started or completed — along with standard device and app information. This helps us understand how the App is used and improve it.

**Photos.** If you choose to set a profile photo, the App requests access to your photo library so you can select an image. We only access the photo you specifically choose.

**Push notification tokens.** If you enable notifications, we collect a push notification token so we can alert you when your itinerary is ready.

We do **not** collect your device's precise GPS location. Destinations are based on the place names and trips you enter, not your physical location.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create, personalize, and improve your travel itineraries;
- Authenticate you and maintain your account;
- Process and manage subscriptions and trip credits, and enforce usage limits;
- Send you notifications you have enabled (such as when a trip is ready);
- Analyze and improve the App's performance and features;
- Detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud, or technical issues.

## How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the App, and only as needed to provide the service:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Cloud Messaging, App Check, Storage) — hosting, accounts, data storage, analytics, and notifications.
- **Anthropic (Claude API)** — your trip details and prompts are sent to Anthropic's API to generate itineraries. This content is processed to produce your trip and is subject to Anthropic's terms.
- **Google Places API** — destination and place searches are sent to Google to retrieve real venue, restaurant, and attraction data.
- **RevenueCat, Apple, and Google** — to process and manage in-app purchases and subscriptions.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a business transfer.

## Data Retention

We retain your account and trip data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see "Your Rights" below).

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to restrict or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights — including deleting your account and associated data — contact us at **wanderlyapp01@gmail.com**. You can also stop all collection by uninstalling the App.

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, our legal bases for processing are performance of our contract with you (providing the App), your consent (for example, notifications), and our legitimate interests (improving and securing the App).

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures, including Firebase security rules and App Check, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## International Users

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App you consent to this transfer.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
